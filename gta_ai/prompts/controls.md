## Control cheat sheet (PlayStation layout)

On foot:
- Left stick: move. Right stick: camera. l3: crouch / stealth.
- cross: sprint (tap repeatedly or hold). square: jump / climb.
- triangle: enter or exit a vehicle (also usually "interact").
- circle: melee / reload. r1: take cover.
- l2: aim. r2: shoot or attack while aiming. Hold l1: weapon wheel.
- dpad_up: phone. dpad_right: context interact or accept. dpad_down: switch character (if offered).
- touchpad: map. options: pause menu (avoid).

In a vehicle:
- r2: accelerate. l2: brake / reverse. Left stick x: steer.
- r1: handbrake (good for sharp turns). triangle: exit the vehicle.
- l3: horn. r3: look behind. Right stick: camera.
- dpad_left / dpad_right: change radio station.

Tips:
- To steal a car, stand next to the driver door and tap triangle.
- To turn around on foot, hold the left stick in the new direction. The camera follows when you move the right stick.
- Waypoints and mission markers show on the minimap (bottom-left). Steer so the route line points up on the minimap.
