# 07 — Go-to-Market

## 1. Positioning
**"Notice the moment. Nudge helps you catch the habit before it catches you."**
A discreet wrist band that gently nudges you when your hand starts the motion of
picking, pulling, or biting — and quietly tracks your progress. Awareness, not
nagging. Private by design.

Anchor against the incumbent without naming it negatively: *more gestures, longer
battery, friendlier price, your data stays on the device.*

## 2. Who you sell to first (the engaged core)
The BFRB community is small, tight, and vocal — perfect for a proof batch.
- **Communities:** TLC Foundation for BFRBs, r/trichotillomania,
  r/Dermatillomania, r/CalmHands, Discords, and BFRB-focused therapists/coaches.
- **Influencers/advocates:** creators who openly talk about skin-picking/hair-
  pulling. A few authentic shout-outs outperform paid ads here.
- **Therapists doing HRT/CBT:** the device supports the "awareness training" step
  — a clinician channel is high-trust and repeatable.

Lead with **empathy and honesty**, never shame. This community has been burned by
gimmicks; under-promise, over-deliver, and be transparent about accuracy.

## 3. Channels & sequence
1. **Proof (now, 10–25 units):** sell/loan to pilot users via a simple landing
   page + **Tindie** (maker-friendly, no MOQ) or direct invoice. Goal = testimony
   + retention data, not profit.
2. **Pre-orders / Kickstarter:** once the design holds up for 2+ weeks on real
   wrists, run a modest crowdfunder. The BFRB story + honest demo video funds the
   first real PnP panel run and the aluminum mold.
3. **Direct DTC (Shopify) + Tindie/Etsy:** steady channel post-campaign.
4. **Clinician / B2B2C:** therapists buy in small lots for clients.

## 4. Pricing & unit economics
- **Retail target: $79–99** (vs incumbent $129–149).
- **Proto cost** ≈ $40/unit all-in (qty 25); **margin ≈ $40–55/unit** even at
  proof scale.
- Optional **subscription-light**: free on-device app; optional cloud backup /
  advanced insights later — keep v1 simple and one-time-purchase to build trust.
- **Income math:** at ~$45 margin, **~5 units/week ≈ $225/week**. The crowdfunder
  is the step-change; the community + clinician channel is the steady base.

## 5. Claims & copy guardrails (legal-critical — mirrors `01_PRD.md` §6)
- ✅ Allowed: "build awareness", "notice the motion", "track your habit", "support
  your awareness training", "general wellness".
- ❌ Forbidden everywhere (site, box, app, ads): "treat/cure/therapy for
  trichotillomania/dermatillomania", "diagnose", "medical", "clinically proven"
  (unless you actually run a trial). Disease claims = FDA medical-device
  jurisdiction. One regulatory-attorney hour before paid launch.
- Include an insert: *"Nudge is a general wellness device for awareness and habit
  tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any
  condition. If you're struggling, consider working with a qualified clinician
  (e.g., the TLC Foundation for BFRBs has a directory)."* — honest *and*
  protective, and the community respects it.

## 6. Privacy as a feature
Mental-health-adjacent data is sensitive. **On-device-first** is both ethical and
a selling point: "Your habit data lives on your wrist, not in our cloud." If you
add cloud later, make it opt-in, aggregate, encrypted, and clearly disclosed
(US state consumer-health-data laws + GDPR).

## 7. Brand & content
- Name check: ensure "Nudge" (or your final name) is clear of trademark conflicts
  in wearables/health before printing anything. Have a backup name.
- Content that converts here: an **honest accuracy demo** (show a false positive
  and how you tuned it out), real user diaries, and the "your data stays local"
  story. Avoid hype reels.

## 8. Roadmap after proof
- v1.1: rigid-flex PCB, molded band, iOS app (Flutter), multi-gesture profiles.
- v2: optional EDA/skin-temp for context; sleep-mode picking detection; clinician
  dashboard (opt-in); retail-ready packaging.
- Moat compounding: every fielded unit's (consented) false-alarm marks retrain
  the model you push via OTA — accuracy improves for everyone over time.
