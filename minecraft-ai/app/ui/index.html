Quick magic

Each friend is an AI player that joins your world. Talk to them in chat: "Buddy, come here", "can you get us wood?", "help me build a house". With more than one friend, say their name so they know who you mean.

Drop extra mod .jar files into the mods folder, then press Stop and Play. Each world has its own save.

Your keys are saved only on this computer (in data/config.json). You only need a key for the AI companies your friends use.

Changes to difficulty, memory and family-only apply the next time you press Play.
